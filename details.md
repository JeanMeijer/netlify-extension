# Simple Analytics

[Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/?utm_source=netlify&utm_content=dashboard) is a privacy-first alternative to Google Analytics. It uses no cookies and no trackers, so visitors don't have to give consent. The free plan stores 30 days of data, and paid plans offer more features.

You never need to enter a Simple Analytics site ID. The extension automatically identifies your website by its hostname.

## How it works

1. Install the extension on your Netlify team.
2. Open a Netlify project and select **Simple Analytics** in the project navigation.
3. Enable Simple Analytics and save the settings.
4. Trigger a production deploy.
5. Visit your website to start collecting analytics.
6. Open the [Simple Analytics dashboard](https://dashboard.simpleanalytics.com/?utm_source=netlify&utm_content=dashboard) to create or sign in to your account, add your domain, and view your analytics.
